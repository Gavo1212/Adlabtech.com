The Challenge

Amara Foods had strong word-of-mouth but no website, so most people discovering them online just saw an inactive social page and had no easy way to see the menu or place an order.

The Approach

We built a single-page site centred on the menu and an always-visible order button, using warm photography-style placeholders and a simple structure that loads fast on mobile data.
