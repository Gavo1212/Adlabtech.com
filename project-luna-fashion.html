The Challenge

The brand's products were being sold only through Instagram DMs, which meant lost sales whenever the founder was offline and no easy way to browse the full catalogue.

The Approach

We designed a clean product-grid storefront with a simple checkout flow and consistent brand styling carried over from their Instagram, so the store feels like a natural extension of the brand.
